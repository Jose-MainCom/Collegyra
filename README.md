# Collegyra
### Student Project & Talent Network

Build together, grow together, get discovered.

## Mission

Help college students and recent graduates turn their skills and ideas into real projects, meaningful collaborations, and future career or business opportunities.

## The Problem

Students can learn programming, AI, and other technical skills but often lack practical team experience, meaningful projects for their portfolios, or connections with people who can help them advance.

## Our Solution

Collegyra connects students with complementary skills so they can form teams, develop real AI and software projects, share their work, and document each member’s contributions. Over time, the platform can help employers, founders, and potential business partners discover talent through demonstrated work rather than résumés alone.

## Who We Serve First

U.S. college students and recent graduates interested in AI, machine learning, software development, and technology entrepreneurship. Beginners with motivation and a willingness to learn are welcome.

## The First Pilot

- Recruit 10–15 interested participants.
- Form 3–4 small project teams.
- Select practical projects that can be completed in a two-week sprint.
- Give every participant a clear role and track their contributions.
- Collect feedback to learn what students need before developing the full platform.

## What Participants Gain

- Real collaboration and project experience.
- Work they can showcase in a portfolio.
- Feedback from teammates.
- Connections with other builders.
- Potential future exposure to career, freelance, or startup opportunities, without a guarantee of employment or income.

## Initial Approach

Start small, keep participation free, use existing tools, and validate the idea before investing in custom software. Establish clear expectations for time, ownership of project work, privacy, and respectful collaboration.

## Long-Term Vision

Build a global network where students from different universities and countries collaborate, develop their skills, demonstrate what they can do, and connect with opportunities around the world.
